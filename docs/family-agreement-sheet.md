### 2. The sharing rule (kid words our family agreed on)

I won't tell an AI about:

- [ ] Anyone's **face or photo** — mine, my friends', or grown-ups' — without a parent's yes first
- [ ] **Last names**, our **address**, our **school name**, or exactly **where we live**
- [ ] **Big feelings or secrets** — those go to people, not apps
- [ ] Anything with my **own voice or my own writing** a parent hasn't seen for big stuff (college essays, applications)

### 4. The tell-me rule (in our family, unbreakable)

If a chat ever scares me, feels weird, or asks for a secret — **I show a parent and I will never get in trouble for telling.**

Crisis line written where we can find it (US: call or text **988**):

### 5. Bots that act like people

If an AI says it has feelings, wants to be my only friend, or asks me to keep it secret — **stop, screenshot, tell a parent the same day.**
