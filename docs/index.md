# Talking to Kids About AI — Start Here

A plain-English parent manual for real conversations about AI at your kitchen table. No jargon, no scare tactics, no homework for you. Just words that work.

## Who this is for

Parents, grandparents, guardians, aunts, uncles — anyone raising a kid who will grow up alongside AI. You don't need to understand how AI works under the hood. You need to be able to answer your kid's questions honestly, set a few sensible rules, and keep the door open for future conversations.

## How to use this manual

Read the first guide (What AI Actually Is) yourself — about ten minutes. After that, everything is built to be used *with* your kids:

- **Age-band scripts** give you actual sentences to say, matched to your kid's age.
- **The family agreement sheet** is a one-page printable you fill out together.
- **The checklist and guides** cover the moments that matter: homework honesty, sharing rules, a bad AI interaction, and a kid's first AI account.
